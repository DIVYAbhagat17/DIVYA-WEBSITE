## 💜 About This Website

Welcome to my personal website!

This website is a reflection of who I am, what I have learned, and the experiences that have shaped my journey. It brings together my academic progress, personal interests, achievements, certifications, and memorable moments in one place.

More than just a website, it is a digital space that grows along with me.

## 🌸 Explore My Website

| Section | Description |
|---|---|
| 🏠 **Home** | A welcome page introducing my digital space. |
| 🎓 **Academics** | A look at my academic journey and progress. |
| 🏆 **Achievements** | Highlights of my accomplishments and recognitions. |
| 📜 **Certifications** | Courses, certifications and learning experiences. |
| 📸 **Memories** | A collection of special moments and experiences. |

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **HTML5** – Structure of the website.
- **CSS3** – Styling, layouts and visual design.
- **JavaScript** – Interactive website functionality, where used.

## ✨ Design Highlights

- Clean and visually appealing interface.
- Simple navigation between different sections.
- Image gallery to showcase memories.
- Organized presentation of academic and personal information.
- A design that can grow as I continue my journey.
